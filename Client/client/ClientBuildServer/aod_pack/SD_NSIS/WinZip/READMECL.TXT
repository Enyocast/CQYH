*** Requirements ***

    This WinZip add-on requires WinZip 12.0 or later and Windows(R)
    2000, Windows XP, Windows Vista (R), Windows 7 or Windows 8.

*** Installation ***

    Double click on the distribution file and follow the prompts.

*** Using the Add-On ***

    The help file WZCLINE.CHM contains complete information
    about using the add-on.  You can view this from Windows
    Explorer.

*** Uninstallation ***

    Windows 2000 and Windows XP
    Activate the Control Panel, double click Add/Remove Programs, click
    the WinZip Command Line Support Add-On entry in the list of
    programs, and click Remove.

    Windows Vista and Windows 7
    Activate the Control Panel, click "Uninstall a program" (located
    under Programs), click the WinZip Command Line Support Add-On entry
    in the list of programs, and click Uninstall.
